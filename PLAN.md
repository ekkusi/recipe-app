Recipe and shopping list app

Features:
- View and add recipes
    - Ingredients and their quantities
        - Choose quantity unit: grams and liters and their variants AND tea spoon/table spoon etc
        - Insert quantity
        - Option to add without quantity (like spices)
    - Instructions
    - Tag, like vegan/lactose-free etc.
    - Difficulty/required time
- View and add shopping list
    - Ingredients and their quantities, similar to recipes

Design:
- Pastel-colors and soft and round
- Not the most typical modern UI. Something a bit different. You can use your creativity and we can work on it later

Infrastructure:
- NextJS for the app and try make it PWA
- ShadCN for components and TailwindCSS
- Setup Supabase for db
- Setup Clerk for authentication
